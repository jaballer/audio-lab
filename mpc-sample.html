Help

  • Hit the pads on the device: they light up here. The page makes no sound, the device plays its own samples
  • The number in each pad is the note it sends in the current bank
  • Click a pad to pick it, then turn its step keys on. Press Play on the device: this page follows its clock and sends it a note for each step that is on, so it plays its own sounds. The steps are not saved yet
  • Notes for the device to play need Pad MIDI In set to On there. It sends each one back as a pad hit, and the pad lights here
  • MIDI monitor shows every raw message
  • No pad events? On the device set MIDI Port to USB and Pad MIDI Out to Always (Shift + Pad 8, MIDI Config)
  • Steps not moving? They run only while the device plays and sends its clock: press Play on the device. Set Bars, Beats and Note to match the sequence there. The page sends no clock of its own
