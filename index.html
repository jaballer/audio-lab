Help
  • Click the pads, or play them with the keys 1–4 Q–R A–F Z–V
  • Space plays or stops the sequence; Stop pressed again, when it is already stopped, cuts off all sound, even what is still ringing
  • Song, at the end of the row with Rec, Play and Stop, plays the song: lit, Play begins at the entry selected in the Song panel (the first, to begin with), each pass (the whole of a sequence, all its bars) is followed by the next of the song, and the song stops after its last pass (the display says END OF SONG), or with Loop on the last is followed by the first, until you press Stop. The display changes to say where the song is (its entry, the sequence and the time round it, and the sequence that comes next, or END), and the sequence keys stay where they are: a press on one only shows that sequence, to edit it, and changes nothing about what plays (the Next key is for when Song is off). Turn Song off while it plays and what plays goes on as a loop, with the keys moving to it. Song is not kept: it is off each time the page opens, and a song that plays does not save the beat as it goes
  • Song, under the pads, is the order the Song key plays: a cell for each entry, with its sequence and how many times it goes round (×3). Until you change it, it is every sequence once, in order, and it follows the sequences you add or remove. Press a cell to select it (the arrow keys, Home and End move along the row): Play begins there when Song is on, and the tools below act on it. Add Entry puts the sequence showing in after it, Remove Entry takes it out (a song keeps one), Move Earlier and Move Later change the order, and Repeats − and + set how many times it goes round, from 1 to 16, with up to 32 entries. Loop, beside the heading, makes the song go round again at its end instead of stopping (it is kept with the beat, off to begin with). While Song plays, the entry that plays has a triangle and shows its time round (2/3), a moment ahead of the sound (about a quarter of a second at 100 BPM). A change is heard from the end of the pass, and the song is kept with the beat. Export saves it: its Song choice has every entry in order, as often as it repeats
  • The two bars at the right of the display show how loud the left and right of the output are, as it is heard (after the soft clipper); the top box lights when the sound is pressed against the clipper's ceiling
  • Drop an audio file on a pad to play it there (up to 10 seconds and 5 MB); it is kept in this browser. Edit Pad opens the pad editor for the selected pad (so does Shift with a click on a pad, or with its key: the pad is selected, and not played): Load Sample, Default Sound, the waveform of a loaded sample with its Start and End (drag the picture, or use the sliders), Gain (for any sound), Pitch and an Envelope of Attack, Decay, Sustain and Release (for a loaded sample, drawn on its waveform: Release is also how long a hit cuts off the pad's last one), and Choke (a group number: pads in the same group cut each other off, like a closed hat cutting an open one) and Color (a colour for the pad's rim, to group pads by sight); press Delete or double-click a slider to reset it. Escape, pressed inside the editor, closes it
  • Under the display, each sequence has a key: press one to see and edit it. While the beat plays (and Song is off), a press makes that sequence play next instead: its key is ringed and blinks (just ringed if the page is set for less motion), and it takes over where the pass ends (the whole of the sequence showing, all its bars) while the one showing plays out; press the same key again, or the one showing, to take that back, and hold Shift to switch at once. Next, at the left of the keys on the right, is the latch for that: pressed in (dark, as it is until you turn it off), a press waits for the end of the pass, as described; raised (light), a press switches at once and Shift waits (a touch screen has no Shift, so raised is how to switch at once there, and the page keeps it). Stopped, a press shows the sequence at once either way. + adds an empty sequence at the end (there can be eight), Copy adds a copy of the one showing, and − removes the one showing (it asks first when it has steps, it cannot be undone, and a beat keeps at least one). Under them, Bars sets how long the sequence is, and the arrows choose which bar the step keys show
  • Ctrl/⌘ Z undoes a change to the steps, with Shift it redoes; Copy Steps and Paste Steps copy the bar showing for the selected pad onto another bar, pad or sequence: pick the next bar with the arrows and paste to repeat it
  • The heading above the step keys hides or shows the step editor (the keys, the velocity bars and the edit tools), and the page remembers which; Tune and Envelope in the pad editor hide and show the same way. Press the Velocity switch to show the velocity bars under the step keys (they start hidden), then drag along them to set how hard each step plays; pressing the bar of a step that is off turns it on at that height; a step recorded live keeps the velocity of the hit
  • Export, in the File group above the display, opens a window to save a 16-bit WAV file, at the tempo, volume and pad settings on the page. Sequences chooses the one showing, or the Song: the order and repeats of the Song panel, as Song mode plays them (an empty sequence as silence), with a few seconds on the end for what rings on (a song longer than 6 min 40 s is too long to save). For the one showing you can choose 1, 2, 4 or 8 loops of it in one file: one loop is exactly the length of its bars, so that it loops, and what rings on past the end is heard at the start. Stereo or mono, with its length and size shown before you save it (and a warning when it needs a lot of memory). Stems makes a WAV for each pad that has steps, in one zip: they are not clipped, and they add up to the mix, so the parts can be mixed elsewhere. It shows how far it has got, and Cancel export or Escape stops it
  • Keyboard test is a page for trying a MIDI keyboard
