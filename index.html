Help

  • Click the pads, or play them with the keys 1–4 Q–R A–F Z–V
  • Space plays or stops the sequence; Stop pressed again, when it is already stopped, cuts off all sound, even what is still ringing
  • Drop an audio file on a pad to play it there (up to 10 seconds and 5 MB); it is kept in this browser. Edit Pad opens the pad editor for the selected pad: Load Sample, Default Sound, the waveform of a loaded sample with its Start and End (drag the picture, or use the sliders), Gain (for any sound), Pitch and an Envelope of Attack, Decay, Sustain and Release (for a loaded sample, drawn on its waveform: Release is also how long a hit cuts off the pad's last one), and Choke (a group number: pads in the same group cut each other off, like a closed hat cutting an open one) and Color (a colour for the pad's rim, to group pads by sight); press Delete or double-click a slider to reset it. Escape, pressed inside the editor, closes it
  • Ctrl/⌘ Z undoes a change to the steps, with Shift it redoes; Copy Steps and Paste Steps copy the bar showing for the selected pad onto another bar, pad or sequence: pick the next bar with the arrows and paste to repeat it
  • The heading above the step keys hides or shows the step editor (the keys, the velocity bars, the bar arrows and the edit tools), and the page remembers which; Tune and Envelope in the pad editor hide and show the same way. Drag along the velocity bars under the step keys to set how hard each step plays; a step recorded live keeps the velocity of the hit
  • Export WAV saves the sequence showing as a 16-bit stereo WAV file, exactly the length of its bars so that it loops, at the tempo, volume and pad settings on the page; what rings on past the end is heard at the start
  • Keyboard test is a page for trying a MIDI keyboard
